Privacy Policy — Kam Accounting Bot

Last updated: October 10, 2026

What the app is

Kam Accounting Bot is a private app that runs only on its owner's own computer. There is no server, no website login and no user accounts. Nobody other than the owner uses it.

Google data the app uses

When the owner connects their Google account, the app asks for permission to:

  • Google Sheets: read the owner's form responses sheet and write the report spreadsheet.
  • Google Drive: create the report in the owner's reports folder and remove an unfinished report if a run fails.
  • Gmail (send only): email the finished report link to recipients the owner chooses. The app cannot read the owner's email.

This data is used only to build and deliver the owner's report.

Where data is kept

The Google sign-in and the app's settings are stored only on the owner's computer. The app does not send Google data to the developer or to any third party, does not sell it, and does not use it for advertising or to train AI models. Other than Google's own services, the only outside service the app contacts is the owner's sales platform (Whop), to read the owner's own sales records.

Google API Services User Data Policy

The app's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

The owner can disconnect the app at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions, and can delete the app's folder from their computer to remove everything it stored.
